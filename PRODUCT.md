# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React with Vite for a responsive client-side storefront; no backend or payment integration is in scope for this first iteration.

## Users

People interested in streetwear, urban culture, and distinctive/upcycled clothing, browsing the ARQUIVO061 collection on desktop or mobile. This audience is inferred from the provided brand materials and should be confirmed by the brand owner.

## Product Purpose

An online storefront for ARQUIVO061 where visitors can browse clothing, choose a size and quantity, and assemble a cart summary. Success for the first release means a visitor can understand the brand, explore the available pieces, and review a draft order.

## Positioning

ARQUIVO061 presents streetwear connected to the culture and territory of the Federal District, with upcycling and reuse of textile deadstock as a core brand idea. This positioning is grounded in the supplied reference boards and website copy.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the collection, select a size for each product, adjust quantities, and review their cart. The cart is stored locally in the visitor's browser; there are no accounts or server-side orders in this release.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product catalogue with editable demonstration names, prices, size options, and availability. Initial sample size grid: PP, P, M, G, GG.
- Product size selection, quantity controls, cart drawer/page, subtotal, and item removal.
- Cart persistence using browser local storage.
- No payment, shipping calculation, account, order submission, or backend integration in this release.
- Initial fictional product names/prices for the supplied photos: Moletom Bloco 061 (quarter-zip color-block) — R$ 289; Camiseta Arquivo Fantasma — R$ 119; Moletom Remendo Shark — Vinho (patchwork) — R$ 329; Moletom Remendo Shark — Cru (patchwork) — R$ 329. These are demonstration values, not real offers, and must be confirmed/replaced before launch.
- Demonstration availability is not real inventory and must be replaced before launch.
- Real inventory, final product details, pricing, shipping policy, and payment provider remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: ARQUIVO061.
- Preserve the existing ghost mascot/logo and the supplied identity assets.
- Brand themes: streetwear, urban culture of the DF, reuse/upcycling, and local belonging.
- Reference line from the public brand page: “O que a indústria descarta, a rua transforma em história.”
- Identity material uses black/off-white with lime green, burgundy, and olive accents; supplied product imagery also includes color-block garments.

- Keep the path from product discovery to cart review direct and understandable.
- Treat all demonstration commercial data as replaceable, not as real inventory or offers.
